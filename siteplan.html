Mbabane Chamber of Commerce

Website Site Plan

Site Name

Mbabane Chamber of Commerce

The name "Mbabane Chamber of Commerce" was selected because it clearly identifies the organization, its location, and its purpose. The name is straightforward and recognizable to local businesses, entrepreneurs, residents, and visitors.

The website will serve businesses and organizations in Mbabane by providing information about chamber membership, local businesses, events, and business resources.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Mbabane Chamber of Commerce website is to provide a central online resource for businesses and entrepreneurs in Mbabane, Eswatini.

The website will provide information about the Chamber, membership opportunities, upcoming events, local business resources, and businesses that are members of the Chamber. Visitors will also be able to explore a business directory and learn how to become a member.

The site will help connect local businesses with customers, entrepreneurs, visitors, and other businesses while promoting economic activity within the Mbabane community.

Scenarios

The following scenarios represent questions that members of the target audience may ask when visiting the website.

Scenario 1

What businesses are members of the Mbabane Chamber of Commerce?

Scenario 2

How can my business become a member of the Chamber?

Scenario 3

What upcoming business events and activities are happening in Mbabane?

Scenario 4

How can I contact the Mbabane Chamber of Commerce?

Color Scheme

The color scheme is designed to create a professional, trustworthy, and welcoming appearance while maintaining good readability and visual consistency.

Dark Teal

#1F4E5F

Dark teal will be used for the header, navigation, headings, footer, borders, and other major elements. It represents professionalism, trust, and stability.

Golden Yellow

#F2C14E

Golden yellow will be used for buttons, highlights, calls to action, accents, and selected decorative elements. It provides contrast and draws attention to important information.

White

#FFFFFF

White will be used primarily as a background and for areas where clean separation between content sections is needed.

Typography

The website will use two primary fonts. Georgia will provide a professional and established appearance for headings, while Arial will be used for body text because it is clean and easy to read on different screen sizes.

Georgia — Headings

Georgia will be used for the main page title, section headings, and other important headings throughout the website.

Arial — Body Text

Arial will be used for paragraphs, navigation links, descriptions, form labels, business information, and other general content.

Target Audience

The primary audience includes local business owners, entrepreneurs, organizations, potential Chamber members, customers looking for local businesses, and visitors seeking information about businesses and economic activity in Mbabane.

The site will use clear navigation, readable text, responsive layouts, and concise information so that users can quickly find the information they need.

Planned Website Content

  • Home page with an introduction to the Chamber
  • Business directory
  • Business membership information
  • Chamber events and activities
  • Local business and community information
  • Contact information
  • Information about the Chamber and its purpose
